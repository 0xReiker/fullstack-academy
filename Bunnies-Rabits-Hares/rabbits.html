Rabbits are just rabbits. They're the middle-child of the rabbit-sphere. They are not quite as lovable and cute as their bunny-brethren, but surely are not the evildoers of their kin. They're the rabbit middling-sort; the urban middle strata, the petite bourgeoisie.
