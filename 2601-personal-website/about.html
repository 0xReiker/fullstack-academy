About me

I grew up on a small island in the Puget Sound called Bainbridge Island. It's just a boat ride away from Seattle.
I spent most of my teens and early twenties in hospitals and institutions due to a traumatic brain injury. Thankfully, at 25 I found a specialist who was able to help me get through it. From then on, I've been doing my best to experience all the things I missed out on.

Japan

After things settled down and I got my bearings, I packed my things and moved to Japan. I joined a school in Kyoto, moved into a sharehouse and ended up meeting my wife. We lived in Japan together until her resonsibilities caught up to her and we had to move back for her to continue on her career path to becoming a teacher.

Italy

After getting married, my wife and I moved back to Italy, to a small town called Marino, just outside of Rome. I had fun exploring the country and meeting new people for the first few months, but after a while I realized that I should probably start acting a bit more like an adult and venture into a career that I could enjoy.

Goals for the Future

I found that the thing that I enjoyed the most was programming. I initially started with C, working through the C Programming: A Modern Approach textbook by K. N. King before moving onto JavaScript. I then worked through the JavaScript: The Definitive Guide by Flanagan in preperation for the Fullstack Academy Full-Time course. I don't know if I want to become a web dev, but I know that I love programming and hope to find a career in it once I feel confident in my abilities and feel near fluent in at least one language so that I can have the transferable skills to learn new languages and to explore different paths in the field.
